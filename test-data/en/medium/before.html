How to organize your notes

A good structure helps you find any note in seconds. This article shows three ways to organize notes in Notebook Online: notebooks, tags and pinned notes.

Notebooks

Notebooks group related notes, for example Work, Home or Travel. Each note belongs to exactly one notebook.

To create a notebook, click New notebook in the sidebar and give it a name. You can change the name later.

Tags

Tags work across notebooks. A note can have up to 20 tags, and you can filter the list by several tags at once.

  • Use short, lowercase names, such as invoice or ideas.
  • Avoid tags that repeat a notebook name.
  • Review your tags once a month and merge duplicates.

Pinned notes

Pin the notes you open every day. Pinned notes always stay at the top of the list in every notebook.

Step by step: tidy up your notes

  1. Create a notebook for each area of your life.
  2. Move existing notes to the right notebooks.
  3. Add tags to notes you often search for.
  4. Pin up to five notes.

Tip: you do not have to do it all at once. Start with the notes from the last month.

Frequently asked questions

Can a note be in two notebooks?

No. Use a tag instead, or add a link to the note.

What happens to tags when I delete a notebook?

Tags stay. They disappear only when no note uses them.

Is there a limit on notebooks?

The Free plan allows 10 notebooks. Paid plans have no limit.
