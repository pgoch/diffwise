How to organize your notes

A good structure helps you find any note in seconds. This article shows four ways to organize notes in Notebook Online: notebooks, stacks, tags and pinned notes.

Notebooks

Notebooks group related notes, for example Work, Home or Recipes. Each note belongs to exactly one notebook.

To create a notebook, click New notebook at the top of the sidebar and give it a name.

Stacks

Stacks group notebooks. They help when you have more than ten notebooks.

Tags

Tags work across notebooks. A note can have up to 50 tags, and you can filter the list by several tags at once.

  • Use short, lowercase names, such as invoice or ideas.
  • Review your tags once a month and merge duplicates.
  • Name all tags in one language.

Pinned notes

Pin the notes you open every day.

Step by step: tidy up your notes

  1. Create a notebook for each area of your life.
  2. Move existing notes to the right notebooks.
  3. Add tags to notes you often search for.
  4. Delete notes you no longer need.
  5. Pin up to ten notes.

Tip: you do not have to do it all at once. Start with the notes from the last week.

Frequently asked questions

Can a note be in two notebooks?

No. Use a tag instead, or add a link to the note.

Is there a limit on notebooks?

The Free plan allows 10 notebooks. Paid plans have no limit.

What happens to tags when I delete a notebook?

Tags stay. They disappear only when no note uses them.
