Getting started

Setting up Notebook Online takes about five minutes:

  1. Create an account at example.com/signup.
  2. Confirm your e-mail address. The link is valid for 48 hours.
  3. Turn on two-step verification in Settings → Security.
  4. Install the app on your devices:
    • Windows, macOS and Linux: download it from our website;
    • Android and iOS: get it from your phone’s app store.
  5. Sign in and create your first notebook.

When you are ready, continue with these steps:

Sharing notes

You can share a single note or a whole notebook. Open the note, click Share and enter the e-mail addresses of the people you want to invite.

Roles

  • Viewer – can read notes.
  • Commenter – can read notes and add comments.
  • Editor – can change notes.
  • Owner – can invite others and delete the notebook.

Read more in the sharing guide or ask our support team.

Public links let anyone with the link read the note without signing in. You can turn them off at any time in the note settings.

Formatting

Notes support bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, inline code, subscript and superscript, so you can write H2O or 103.

Important: formatting copied from other apps is kept when you paste it.

Centered text works well for titles and quotes.

Text can have a color or a background.

Tracked changes from other editors are kept as well: old wording final wording.

Press Shift + Enter to start a new line without a new paragraph:

Privacy and data

Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest.1 We never sell your data or show ads.

You can export all your notes as HTML, Markdown or PDF files, or delete your account in Settings → Account. Deleted accounts are removed from backups after 14 days.

Where are your servers?

Our servers are in data centers in Frankfurt and Warsaw.
