NOTEBOOK ONLINE - SYNC SERVICE
==============================

Operations notes, version 3.2
Last update: 2 March 2026
Owner:  platform team (platform@example.com)


1. Overview
-----------

The sync service keeps notes the same on every device of a user. It runs in three regions and stores a copy of each note in at least two of them, so a note stays available when one region goes down. Changes made offline wait on the device and go out as soon as it is online again.
This file is plain text on purpose: it opens in any editor, it reads well in a terminal, and a diff of two versions shows every space and every tab, which matters for the settings below.

3. Sync settings
----------------

The settings below are the defaults for new accounts. Indentation shows which group each setting belongs to.

sync:
  interval: 15 minutes
  wifi only: yes
	background: yes
    retries: 3
backup:
    time: 02:00 UTC
    keep: 30 days

Regions, separated by tabs:
region	nodes	status
eu-west	12	ok
us-east	9	ok
ap-south	4	degraded

5. Known issues
---------------

- Notes with more than 50,000 characters sync slowly.
- Some editors paste text such as <b>bold</b> or <script>alert("hi")</script> into the title. The app must show such text as it is and never run it.
- Titles with &amp; or &lt; show the entity instead of the character in the old Android app.
- Very long links, such as https://example.com/notebooks/shared/2026/03/team-planning/very-long-note-title-that-never-ends, do not wrap on small screens.
    
- The emoji picker 😀 is missing on desktop.


6. Notes for translators
------------------------

Keep the texts of screens short: they must fit on a phone.
Texts of the sync screen in Chinese (simplified characters):
  同步已完成。
  笔记会自动同步到所有设备。
  每天凌晨两点备份一次。
Write dates the way the language of the screen writes them.
